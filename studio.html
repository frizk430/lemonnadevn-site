The Museum

Hip-Hop History You Can Walk Through.

The We Working Hip-Hop Museum at Lemonnade Van Nuys celebrates decades of hip-hop culture through memorabilia collected throughout music executive Steve Lobel's career, along with pieces contributed by artists themselves.

This isn't a collection created overnight. It's a living archive shaped by the people, music and moments that helped define hip-hop. From rare promotional pieces and artist memorabilia to artifacts connected to some of the culture's most recognizable names, every corner tells a story.

Built From A Life In Music

Curated By Steve Lobel

The museum reflects more than three decades of Steve Lobel's career in the music industry. Instead of building a traditional museum and searching for pieces to fill it, Lobel had spent years holding onto promotional items, memorabilia and pieces of music history.

When artists began contributing pieces of their own, the collection continued to grow. Lobel described the process of creating the museum as essentially freestyling it — allowing his experiences and relationships throughout hip-hop to shape the collection.

The Studio

Create Where Culture Lives.

The We Working Studio isn't a display. It's a real, functional recording studio located inside Lemonnade Van Nuys, created to give artists, producers and creatives a place to make music surrounded by the culture that inspired it.

The studio features professional recording equipment, live instruments, an engineer and a raised DJ stage designed for sessions, events and artist experiences.

The Story So Far

Timeline

2021

The Museum Opens

The We Working Hip-Hop Museum takes shape inside Lemonnade, bringing together decades of memorabilia collected by Steve Lobel and pieces contributed by artists.

2021

Music Gets Made

The on-site recording studio becomes part of the Lemonnade experience. Lil' Cease, Layzie Bone and Snow are among the artists who record in the booth.

2021–2022

Artists Come Through

DMC, Dave East, Benny The Butcher, OhGeesy, Krayzie Bone and Conway The Machine are among artists associated with meet-and-greets and visits to the space.

April 2022

Paradigm × AB Biscotti

Antonio Brown brings music and cannabis together at Lemonnade with his Paradigm album event and AB Biscotti launch.

May 2022

Hit-Boy

Grammy-winning producer Hit-Boy appears at Lemonnade while launching Hit-Boy OG, with TMZ interviewing him at the location.
