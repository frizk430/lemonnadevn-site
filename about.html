Van Nuys' dispensary, hip-hop museum, and recording studio — all in one spot.

Lemonnade Van Nuys isn't just a dispensary — it's a full experience. Along with a curated, top-shelf menu that rotates weekly, we house a hip-hop museum with rare memorabilia and vinyl, plus a fully working recording studio where local artists come to write and record.

We serve both recreational and medical customers, 21+ with valid ID — no medical card required.
