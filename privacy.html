Information We Collect

When you browse this site, place an order through our Meadow-powered menu, book a studio session, or contact us, we may collect information such as your name, email, phone number, and order details. Our website may also collect standard technical information (device, browser, pages visited) via analytics.

Age Verification

Because California law restricts cannabis sales to adults 21+ (or medical patients 18+), we confirm age at entry to this site and again with valid ID at time of in-store purchase.

How We Use Information

  • To process and fulfill orders placed through our menu
  • To communicate with you about your order, events, or studio bookings
  • To improve our site, menu, and in-store experience
  • To comply with California cannabis regulatory and record-keeping requirements

Third Parties

Order and menu functionality is powered by Meadow, our point-of-sale provider. Deals, events, and blog content are managed through Supabase. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security practices.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you or to request it be deleted, subject to what California cannabis record-keeping law requires us to retain.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to us via our Contact page.
