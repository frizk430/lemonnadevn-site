21+ Only

Our age policy, in plain terms.

Who Can Purchase

California law requires cannabis retailers to sell only to adults 21 years of age or older for recreational purchases, or to valid California medical cannabis patients 18 years of age or older with a physician's recommendation.

ID Requirements

Valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport) is required on every visit and every order — no exceptions, whether you're ordering online for pickup or shopping in-store.

Why This Site Asks Your Age

The age gate on this website is a first checkpoint required for browsing cannabis retail content online. It does not replace the in-person ID check required at pickup or purchase.
